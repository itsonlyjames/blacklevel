App Icons

A second round, after looking at how award-winning type-led sites put images next to words. The common thread: the type stays in charge, and an image either rides the type at its own scale or appears one at a time, never as a grid of tiles. Click a screen and use the arrow keys like the remote.

Signal-A Studio SOTD

Sets a solid dot as a character, cap-height, inside its display serif. On the archive, one large image appears over the list for the row in focus only.

Kaltpresse SOTD

Small icons hang off the cap line after key words, like footnote marks. They read as part of the sentence, not as UI.

Gionatan Nese SOTD

Superior numerals beside nav words, and a tight strip of thumbnails that works as an index while one item takes the stage.

31 for 31 Nominee

The same 31 films as a Shelf of artwork or a List of type. You pick the view, and the List drops the artwork entirely.
