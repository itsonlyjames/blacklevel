# Design Context

- **Audience**: one household, sitting ~3 m from a 65" Sony A80J OLED, holding a D-pad remote.
- **Use cases**: open a streaming app or switch to an HDMI input (SHIELD) in as few presses as possible; glance at the time.
- **Tone**: "It's just a TV." Quiet, fast, typographic. No ads, no rows of recommendations, no Google/Sony branding.
- **Constraints**: OLED burn-in — dark tinted background, no large static bright areas, dims and drifts when idle. 10-foot readability. Offline (fonts bundled). D-pad only.
